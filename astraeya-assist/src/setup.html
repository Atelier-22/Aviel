Astra Assist

Astraeya on your PC. Turn it on, talk, and it looks at your screen and helps out loud.

Server

No account? Create one in the Astraeya chat first.

You're set.

  • Press Windows + Ctrl + A to turn Astra Assist on, and again to turn it off.
  • Then just talk: "What's wrong with this command?", "Reply to this email professionally".
  • Drafts go to your clipboard: press Ctrl + V to paste.
  • Say "turn off" or press the keys again to stop.

While it's on, each question sends a screenshot of your screen to Astraeya to be read, and your spoken question (and the spoken answer) may be processed by Astraeya's speech provider so it's instant. They're used for that answer only and not stored. Nothing is captured or heard while it's off.
